ARED Coach — exercise recognition with the camera

Why it matters. On the ISS astronauts exercise about two hours a day. A study of 46 astronauts found that more resistive exercise volume (reps × load) was linked to more leg strength and muscle and healthier bones. This coach measures exactly that volume. Coins are earned for every finished set, plus extra for reps with good form.

Video is processed only on this device; it is never saved or sent anywhere. Educational prototype, not a medical device.

Converting between gravities

Joint angles come from anatomy, so they stay the same anywhere. Forces, work and timing change, each with the ratio k = g source / g target. The current set (load, reps, angles, duration) is used automatically.
